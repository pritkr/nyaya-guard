# Mukhyamantri Ration / NFSA PDS (Bihar)

- Department: Food & Consumer Protection Dept, Govt of Bihar
- Coverage: Antyodaya (AAY) 35 kg/month per family; Priority Households (PHH) 5 kg/person/month.
- Price: Free foodgrain under PMGKAY extension as notified; normal NFSA issue price Rs 3/kg rice, Rs 2/kg wheat when central free-grain orders lapse.
- Eligibility: Domicile of Bihar; NFSA ration card holder (AAY or PHH); excluded if income-tax payer, govt employee (regular), 4-wheeler owner, or holding >5 acres irrigated land (per state exclusion list).
- Documents: Aadhaar of family members, residence proof, income/self-declaration.
- Apply: SDO / RTPS counter / JPV portal (sfc.bihar.gov.in). Grievance: toll-free 1800-3456-194.
- Source note: figures mirror NFSA 2013 entitlements + Bihar SFC notices. Verify at sfc.bihar.gov.in before applying.
