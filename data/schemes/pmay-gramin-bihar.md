# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G, Bihar)

- Department: Rural Development Dept, Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 1,20,000 in plain areas for house construction in 3 instalments + Rs 12,000 for toilet (SBM convergence) + 90 days of MGNREGA wage employment; hilly/difficult-area rate Rs 1,30,000 (not applicable in Bihar plains).
- Eligibility: Bihar domicile; houseless or living in kutcha/dilapidated house; listed in SECC 2011 + Awaas+ 2018 survey permanent waitlist; priority to widows, SC/ST, divyang, disaster-affected; excluded if owning a pucca house, motorised vehicle, or paying income tax.
- Documents: Aadhaar, SECC/Awaas+ job reference, bank account, affidavit of houselessness.
- Apply: Block / Panchayat via AwaasApp; list checked at rhreporting.nic.in and the Gram Sabha permanent waitlist.
- Source note: Rs 1,20,000 + Rs 12,000 toilet figures per MoRD guidelines. Verify on pmayg.nic.in.
