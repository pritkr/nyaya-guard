# Mukhyamantri Poshak Yojana (School Uniform, Bihar)

- Department: Education Dept, Govt of Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 1,200 per year (Class 1-5), Rs 1,200 per year (Class 6-8), Rs 1,500 per year (Class 9-12) for uniform, paid via DBT.
- Eligibility: Students of government / government-aided primary, middle, secondary and senior secondary schools of Bihar; Classes 1-12.
- Documents: Aadhaar, school enrolment record, DBT bank account.
- Apply: Through school via MedhaSoft; amount credited before the academic session.
- Source note: slab figures per Education Dept DBT circulars. Verify on medhasoft.bih.nic.in.
