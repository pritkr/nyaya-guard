# Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana (BSCC)

- Department: Education Finance Corp / Labour Resources (Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta family of schemes)
- Benefit: Education loan up to Rs 4,00,000 at simple interest; girls / divyang / transgender borrowers pay 1% interest, others 4%; 12th-pass students only.
- Eligibility: Bihar domicile; passed Class 12 from a recognised board; enrolled in a recognised higher-education / technical / professional course; age up to 25 years (general) at application; co-applicant parent/guardian.
- Repayment: Begins after course completion + 1 year; flexible EMI.
- Documents: Aadhaar, Class 10 & 12 marksheets, admission proof, bank details, income certificate.
- Apply: mnssby.bihar.gov.in (DRCC counter in each district).
- Source note: Rs 4,00,000 ceiling and 1%/4% rates per scheme guidelines. Verify on mnssby.bihar.gov.in.
