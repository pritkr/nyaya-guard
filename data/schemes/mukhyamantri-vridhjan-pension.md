# Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojana (Old Age Pension, Bihar)

- Department: Social Welfare Dept, Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 400/month for age 60-79; Rs 500/month for age 80 and above; paid quarterly via DBT under NSAP + state top-up.
- Eligibility: Bihar domicile; age 60 years or above; not a regular government employee/pensioner; not an income-tax payer; destitute/poor senior citizens prioritised.
- Exclusions: Persons receiving any other regular social-security pension of equal or higher value (one pension only).
- Documents: Aadhaar, age proof (voter ID/birth certificate), bank account, residence proof.
- Apply: RTPS / Block office / elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
- Source note: Rs 400/500 slabs per Social Welfare Dept + NSAP orders. Verify on elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
