# Mukhyamantri Divyangjan Pension (Disability Pension, Bihar)

- Department: Social Welfare Dept, Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 400/month (below 80), Rs 500/month (80+); disability pensioners with 80%+ disability flagged for state top-up proposals where notified.
- Eligibility: Bihar domicile; disability of 40% or above certified by a medical board (UDID card); any age; family income up to Rs 60,000/year for state component prioritisation.
- Documents: UDID/disability certificate (40%+), Aadhaar, bank account, residence proof.
- Apply: RTPS / Block office / elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
- Source note: 40% threshold per RPwD-linked state orders. Verify on elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
