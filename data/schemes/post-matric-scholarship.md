# Post-Matric Scholarship (Bihar, SC/ST/OBC/EBC)

- Department: SC/ST & BC/EBC Welfare Depts, Bihar
- Benefit: Maintenance allowance Rs 3,800-13,500/year by course group (Group I professional courses highest) + compulsory non-refundable fees reimbursed as per state fee structure.
- Eligibility: Bihar domicile; SC/ST/OBC/EBC student passed Class 10, studying Class 11 and above in a recognised institution; family income up to Rs 2,50,000/year (SC/ST central share) and up to Rs 3,00,000/year (BC/EBC state share for state top-up).
- Documents: Caste/income certificates, Aadhaar, Class 10 marksheet, admission receipt, bank account.
- Apply: PMS online portal (pms.bih.nic.in) + NSP for central schemes.
- Source note: income caps per 2023-25 PMS guidelines. Verify on pms.bih.nic.in.
