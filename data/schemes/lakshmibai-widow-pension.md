# Lakshmibai Samajik Suraksha Pension (Widow Pension, Bihar)

- Department: Social Welfare Dept, Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 400/month (below 80 years), Rs 500/month (80+) for widows; paid quarterly via DBT.
- Eligibility: Widow, Bihar domicile; age 18 years or above; annual family income up to Rs 60,000; not remarried; not a government employee or income-tax payer.
- Documents: Death certificate of husband, Aadhaar, bank account, income certificate, residence proof.
- Apply: RTPS / Block office / elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
- Source note: Rs 400/500 slabs and Rs 60,000 income cap per Social Welfare Dept orders. Verify on elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
