# Mukhyamantri Balika Cycle Yojana (Bihar)

- Department: Education Dept, Govt of Bihar
- Benefit: Rs 3,000 one-time assistance for purchase of a bicycle, paid via DBT to the student's (or guardian's) bank account.
- Eligibility: Girl student, domicile of Bihar; enrolled in Class 9 in a government / government-aided / project school of Bihar; must be recorded on the school rolls (MedhaSoft entry).
- Exclusions: Students of private unaided schools; repeaters who already received the benefit once.
- Documents: Aadhaar, school enrolment/bonafide certificate, bank account (DBT-enabled), caste certificate if applicable.
- Apply: Through the school headmaster via MedhaSoft portal; no direct student application.
- Source note: amount and class per Education Dept circulars (Rs 3,000, Class 9). Verify on medhasoft.bih.nic.in.
