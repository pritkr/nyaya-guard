# Mukhyamantri Balika Cycle Yojana (Bihar) — 2021 version [SUPERSEDED]

- Department: Education Dept, Govt of Bihar (2021 circular)
- Benefit (OLD, NO LONGER VALID): Rs 2,500 one-time assistance for purchase of a bicycle, paid via cheque to the school.
- Eligibility (2021 wording): Girl student, domicile of Bihar; enrolled in Class 9 in a government school of Bihar.
- Note: This document is SUPERSEDED. The current scheme pays Rs 3,000 via DBT (see the current mukhyamantri-cycle-yojana doc, valid from 2023-04-01). Do not quote Rs 2,500 as the current amount.
- Source note: 2021 circular, superseded by the 2023-04-01 revision.
