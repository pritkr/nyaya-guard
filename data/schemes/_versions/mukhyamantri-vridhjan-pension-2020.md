# Mukhyamantri Vridhjan Pension Yojana (Old Age Pension, Bihar) — 2020 version [SUPERSEDED]

- Department: Social Welfare Dept, Bihar (2020 order)
- Benefit (OLD, NO LONGER VALID): Rs 300/month for age 60-79; Rs 400/month for age 80 and above; paid in cash at the block office.
- Deadline (EXPIRED): applications under this order closed on 31 March 2022.
- Eligibility (2020 wording): Bihar domicile; age 60 years or above.
- Note: This document is SUPERSEDED. Current slabs are Rs 400/month for age 60-79 and Rs 500/month for age 80 and above, paid quarterly via DBT with no closing date (see the current mukhyamantri-vridhjan-pension doc, valid from 2022-04-01). Do not quote Rs 300 or the 31 March 2022 deadline as current.
- Source note: 2020 order, superseded by the 2022-04-01 revision. Verify on elabharthi.bih.nic.in.
