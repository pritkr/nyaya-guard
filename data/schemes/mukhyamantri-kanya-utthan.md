# Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (MKUY)

- Department: Women & Child Development Corp, Bihar
- Benefit (total up to Rs 50,000 across life stages): Rs 2,000 at birth; Rs 1,000 at age 1 (Aadhaar seeding); Rs 2,000 at age 2 (full immunisation); Rs 5,000 on Class 6-8 completion/intermediate stages; Rs 10,000 after passing Class 10 (unmarried); Rs 25,000 after graduation (unmarried) — Rs 50,000 total on degree completion.
- Eligibility: Girl child, Bihar domicile; first two daughters per family covered for birth-stage benefits; Rs 10,000 component requires unmarried + Class 10 pass from Bihar board; Rs 25,000 requires unmarried + graduation.
- Documents: Birth certificate, Aadhaar, marksheet/degree, bank account, self-declaration of unmarried status.
- Apply: edbc.bih.nic.in / Anganwadi / online MKUY portal.
- Source note: slab figures per WCDC notifications. Verify on wcdc.bihar.gov.in.
