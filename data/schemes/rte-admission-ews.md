# RTE 25% Admission (Bihar Private Schools, EWS/DG)

- Department: Education Dept, Bihar (Right to Education Act 2009, Section 12(1)(c))
- Benefit: Free admission in Class 1 / pre-school in private unaided schools for 25% of seats; tuition reimbursed to the school by the state (approx Rs 9,000-14,000/child/year as per state reimbursement orders).
- Eligibility: Child aged 6-14 years; Bihar domicile; Economically Weaker Section (family income up to Rs 2,00,000/year) or Disadvantaged Group (SC/ST/OBC-NCL, orphan, HIV-affected, divyang, relevant categories); residence within neighbourhood of the school (1-3 km norms).
- Documents: Birth certificate, income/caste certificate, residence proof, Aadhaar of child/guardian.
- Apply: RTE portal of Bihar Education Dept during the admission window (usually Jan-Mar).
- Source note: 25% quota and 6-14 age band per RTE Act. Verify on education.bih.gov.in.
